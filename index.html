Why Choose Us?

Aim

Take and keep girls in school by providing them with adequate resources that would enhance their school life.

Objective

To plant a seed within the mind of girls to rely on education for economic freedom and financial stability.

Goal

To reach young girls across the African continent because education is still considered a luxury, especially for a girl child.

About Us

Girl Child Education is a nonprofit founded by Tshepiso Tsogang in Kuruman, Northern Cape, South Africa, delivering menstrual care and school-support services across rural communities in South Africa and Zimbabwe in partnership with CouldYouCup (USA).

By providing reusable menstrual cups and pads, the organisation keeps girls in school, enables sports participation, reduces the risk of early child marriage and gives girls choice and dignity during their periods.

To date GCE has reached over 20,000 girls and is working toward expanding its impact to all 54 African countries; future plans include building computer labs in rural schools to boost digital literacy and long-term opportunity for girls.

Aligned Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Quality Education

Keeps girls in school, improves attendance and learning outcomes; planned computer labs support digital skills.

Gender Equality

Removes menstrual-related barriers, promotes girls’ empowerment, agency and equal opportunities.

Improves menstrual health and hygiene, reducing infections and related health risks.

Menstrual-care distribution pairs with sanitation and WASH needs essential for safe use and management.

Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
