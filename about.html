About Us

Girl Child Education is a nonprofit founded by Tshepiso Tsogang in Kuruman, Northern Cape, South Africa, delivering menstrual care and school-support services across rural communities in South Africa and Zimbabwe in partnership with CouldYouCup (USA).

By providing reusable menstrual cups and pads, the organisation keeps girls in school, enables sports participation, reduces the risk of early child marriage and gives girls choice and dignity during their periods.

To date GCE has reached over 20,000 girls and is working toward expanding its impact to all 54 African countries; future plans include building computer labs in rural schools to boost digital literacy and long-term opportunity for girls.


The inspiration came from my Great-Grandfather born 1913 who was a former miner worker and believed in the power of education especially for girls. In addition, the women who marched to the Union Buildings in 1956 to fight against the injustice laws that prohibited women and girls from participating in economic activities. Lastly, we want to eliminate the number of women and girls who are vulnerable to the “blesser-sugar daddy” culture.


Our team has male facilitators and mentors that help to support the wellbeing and educational journey of our young boys.

Our Vision, Mission & Values

Building a future where every girl has the opportunity to learn, grow and achieve her dreams.

Our Vision

To see a world where every girl child has equal access to education, opportunities and resources to reach her full potential, regardless of her background or circumstances.

Our Mission

To empower and support girls in underserved communities through menstrual health education, reusable menstrual products, school support and mentorship, helping them stay in school and build brighter futures.

Our Values

We believe in dignity, equality, empowerment, education, compassion and integrity. We are committed to creating safe and supportive environments where girls and young people can thrive.
